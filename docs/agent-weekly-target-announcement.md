## 3. Talking points (meeting or voice note, about 2 minutes)

1. **What changed.** The daily check is gone. Posting new Rent Plans now depends on last week's collections. The week is Thursday to Wednesday.
2. **When it's decided.** Every Thursday morning. The result holds for the whole week, so plan your week, not your day.
3. **The targets.**
   - Current Rent Plans: 50% of what was due that week.
   - Expired Rent Plans, if you also have current ones: 20% of what was due, on top of the 50%.
   - If every plan has expired: 70%.
4. **Each tenant counts up to their own due.** Overpayment from one tenant doesn't cover another. Visit everyone, especially tenants on expired plans.
5. **Where to look.** Menu at the top → Weekly Performance. Show it on a phone: the green/red banner, "This week so far", the amount needed by Wednesday, the tenant list with call buttons.
6. **If you're blocked.** Keep collecting and registering. The page shows exactly what to collect this week so you can post again from next Thursday.
7. **First week.** This week's result (22–28 October) decides posting from Thursday 29 October.

## 4. Questions and answers

**Why can't I post today? I collected well yesterday.**
Posting now depends on the whole of last week (Thursday to Wednesday), not on one day. Open Weekly Performance to see last week's result and what to collect this week.

**What is a "current" and an "expired" Rent Plan?**
A current Rent Plan is still inside its repayment period. An expired Rent Plan has gone past its last scheduled day but the tenant is still paying. Each plan stays in one group for the whole week, based on where it was on Thursday.

**My tenant paid double. Why doesn't it count fully?**
Each tenant counts only up to what they were due that week, so one tenant's extra payment can't hide another tenant who paid nothing. The extra is still credited to the tenant's Rent Plan as normal.

**I have no current Rent Plans, only expired ones. What do I need?**
70% of what your expired Rent Plans were due last week.

**When will I be able to post again?**
On the next Thursday after a week in which you met the target. The page shows "Next result on Thursday …" and the amount still needed.

**Can Agent Ops unlock me for a day?**
No. Day unlocks ended with the daily check. The weekly result applies to everyone the same way.

**Does this change my commission or the tenant's Rent Plan?**
No. Only the rule for posting new Rent Plans changed.

**What does "Expected this week" on the page mean?**
It's what your tenants will be due for the full week if each tenant keeps being billed the same daily amount until Wednesday. Your target is a share of that.

**I registered a new tenant this week. Do they count?**
A tenant counts from the day their Rent Plan starts being billed, which is the day after the plan is funded.
