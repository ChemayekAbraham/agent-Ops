# Tenant Operations Workspace — User Manual

*Find it at: Tenant Ops → Classic → Workspaces → **Tenant Operations Workspace**.*

Four tabs. Nothing here changes payments, rent, or approvals by itself — it shows what's true and lets you act on it.

## Tab 1: Tenant Top-Up Eligibility

Shows which tenants have paid enough of their current cycle to qualify for a bigger Rent Plan ("top-up"). Cards summarize the list: total tenants, how many are Eligible, and how many sit at each level (100%, 50%, 25%). The donut chart shows the same split — green is best-off, red is "not eligible yet."

Each row shows a tenant's rent, what they owed and paid, and a colored **Eligibility** badge. Click a row for their full story. Filter by level. **Thresholds** (management only) changes the qualifying percentages.

**Example:** a tenant at 92% Paid with a green badge qualifies today; one at 40% needs to pay more first.

**Professional PDF** / **Export Excel** download the full list with totals and percentages per level.

## Tab 2: Tenant Communications

Every tenant payment triggers an automatic SMS with the amount paid, what's left, and top-up status. This tab shows the templates (on/off), a live preview (search a tenant to see their exact message), payment codes, and the care numbers printed in every message.

**Recent payment messages** logs what was sent: green Sent, red Failed, amber Skipped (hover for why). The chart shows volume by channel, last 7 days. Export as PDF or Excel.

## Tab 3: Agent Registration Control

Agents who didn't collect enough of last month's dues are temporarily blocked from registering new tenants. Cards and chart show how many are Blocked (red), on Override (amber), or Clear (green).

If a blocked agent should keep registering, click **Allow anyway** and give a reason (10+ characters) — logged permanently in **Override record** below, with who approved it and when. **Withdraw** ends an override early. **Rules** (management only) sets thresholds. Export both tables.

## Tab 4: Management Overview

Every tenant and agent together, using the *exact same numbers* as Tabs 1 and 3 — nothing recalculated. Switch **Tenants** / **Agents** sub-tabs.

**Tenants**: filter by agent, eligibility, cycle, arrears, or amount paid — active filters show as removable chips. The chart groups the filtered list by tier. Each row's menu (⋯) opens payment history, arrears detail, or transfers the tenant to another agent.

**Agents**: portfolio totals, collected vs. expected, arrears, registration standing, plus a performance-band chart (80%+, 50–79%, under 50%).

Long lists load in batches — **Show more** reveals the rest. Exports always cover everyone, not just what's loaded.

## Exporting and phones

Every tab has **Professional PDF** and **Export Excel** — both cover the full filtered list, not just the visible page. PDFs are branded and paginated with a totals/percentage summary before the detail tables, ready for management. Excel files have multiple sheets (Summary plus data) with frozen headers. On a phone, every table becomes a stack of cards instead of one you scroll sideways through.
