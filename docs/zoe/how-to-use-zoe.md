# How to Use Zoe — Welile Staff Guide

Zoe is your **read-only My Space assistant**. She helps you check official Welile numbers, explore data across your department, think through operational options as a consultant, and draft messages or checklists.

---

## 1. What Zoe Can Do for You

- **Answer your own My Space questions** (any staff member): payslips, salary advances, attendance, leave balances, tasks, HR tickets, requisitions, assets, and canteen menu.
- **Read official department reports** (based on your role): Tenant Ops, Landlord Ops, Calling Center, Agent Ops, Partner Ops (Supporters), HR, Financial Ops, CFO, COO, and CEO.
- **Answer ad-hoc data questions** (*Analyst lane*): when no standard report covers your question, Zoe can run a read-only check on approved `zoe_ro` views for your role and label the answer *"Ad hoc, not certified"*. Click **Show the data** under the answer to inspect the exact table and SQL.
- **Give structured advice & review plans** (*Consultant mode*): ask *"why"*, *"what should we do"*, *"way forward"*, or *"review our plan"*. Zoe will lay out:
  1. **What I looked at** (data source, period, and caveats)
  2. **What the data shows** (tagged `[Evidence]`, `[Assumption]`, or `[Judgement]`)
  3. **Options** (2 or 3 options with trade-offs, risks, and what would change the answer)
  4. **Recommendation and what to measure next**
- **General work help**: ask Zoe to explain a Welile metric, draft a WhatsApp reminder, prepare a meeting agenda, or review an idea in plain text.
- **Files only when you ask**: Zoe never clutters your chat with unrequested downloads. When an answer has a table, tap the small **Excel (.xlsx)** or **PDF** chip—or ask *"give me an Excel"* / *"PDF please"*—to download a clean file.

---

## 2. What Zoe Will Never Do (Safety Rules)

- **Zoe never changes Welile business data.** She cannot approve a requisition, move money, edit a wallet or ledger row, or change a Rent Plan, Supporter portfolio, tenant, or landlord record.
- **Zoe never sends an email or notification without your click.** When you ask her to email a report, set a reminder, notify a colleague, or save an idea, she shows a draft card first so you can review and click **Send** or **Save**.
- **Zoe only shows data your role is allowed to see.** Staff roles require two-step login (authenticator app) before Zoe reads department reports.

## 3. 20 Example Questions You Can Ask Zoe

### Your Own My Space (All Staff)
1. *"Show my recent payslips and net pay in My Space."*
2. *"How many pending tasks do I have in My Work today?"*
3. *"What is my attendance and leave summary for this month?"*
4. *"Check the status of my submitted staff requisitions."*

### Tenant Ops, Landlord Ops & Calling Center
5. *"What is the Tenant Ops First 7 Days summary as of today?"*
6. *"Show the Tenant Ops arrears ageing breakdown across 1-7, 8-14, 15-30, and 30+ days."*
7. *"How many landlords were verified and paid out in Landlord Ops this week?"*
8. *"Show the weekly Calling Center report for Jane, Winnie, and Hanisha."*

### Agent Ops, Supporters (Partner Ops) & HR
9. *"How many silent field collectors do we have today in Agent Ops, and how much rent is expected on their plans?"*
10. *"Compare Wakiso vs Mukono agent daily collections and on-schedule coverage."*
11. *"How many pending Supporter portfolios are in the Partner Ops queue, and what is their total UGX?"*
12. *"How many HR staff contracts are expiring within the next 30 and 60 days?"*

### Finance, COO & CEO
13. *"What is our spendable Treasury Cash Position (Accounts A1 and A5) versus Agent Float (Account A2) today?"*
14. *"Show total Rent Plan receivables versus company payables today."*
15. *"How long have open Rent Plan requests been waiting on each approval desk?"*

### Consultant Mode, Definitions & General Work Help
16. *"Why is uncollected rent high in the First 7 Days report, and what is the best way forward?"*
17. *"Why are Rent Plan requests waiting longest on Landlord Ops, and what should we do this week?"*
18. *"Review our plan for handling the 'Check first' flagged rows in the Landlord Calling Hub and give options."*
19. *"Explain the difference between Expected vs Collected rent in Welile."*
20. *"Help me draft a 15-minute morning standup agenda for the Tenant Ops team — just text in chat, no file please."*

## 4. Giving Feedback or Reporting a Problem

Under every Zoe answer you will see three buttons:
- **Thumbs up** — tells us the answer was helpful.
- **Thumbs down** — lets you add a short note on what was missing.
- **Report a problem** — flags an answer for the weekly manager review list so we can improve Zoe's reports and glossary.
