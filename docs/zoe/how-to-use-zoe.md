# How to Use Zoe — Welile Staff Guide

Zoe is your **read-only My Space assistant**. She helps you check official Welile numbers, explore data across your department, search the Curated Welile Knowledge Base, think through operational options as a consultant, and handle general accounting, writing, and work questions.

---

## 1. What Zoe Can Do for You

- **Answer your own My Space questions** (any staff member): payslips, salary advances, attendance, leave balances, tasks, HR tickets, requisitions, assets, and canteen menu.
- **Read official department reports** (based on your role): Tenant Ops, Landlord Ops, Calling Center, Agent Ops, Partner Ops (Supporters), HR, Financial Ops, CFO, COO, and CEO.
- **Personalised starters & grounded follow-ups**:
  - When you open a new chat, Zoe shows up to **3 personalised starter prompts** based on your own recent question topics (last 30 days) and the tools your role can run.
  - After every data answer, Zoe suggests up to **3 follow-up questions** grounded in the columns and dimensions of that answer (for example *"Break this down by agent"*, *"Compare this vs last week"*, or *"Show only Rent Plans below target"*).
- **Search the Curated Welile Knowledge Base**: Zoe searches Welile's curated glossary, product/policy summaries, process explanations, *"where do I find X"* guides, and measurement gotchas (Expected vs Collected, Treasury Cash `A1 + A5` vs `A2`, Active Rent Plan repayment clock) before ever touching the web, and cites the exact entry.
- **Answer ad-hoc data questions** (*Analyst lane*): when no standard report covers your question, Zoe can run a read-only check on approved `zoe_ro` views for your role and label the answer *"Ad hoc, not certified"*. Click **Show the data** under the answer to inspect the exact table and SQL.
- **Give structured advice & review plans** (*Consultant mode*): ask *"why"*, *"what should we do"*, *"way forward"*, or *"review our plan"*. Zoe will lay out:
  1. **What I looked at** (data source, period, and caveats)
  2. **What the data shows** (tagged `[Evidence]`, `[Assumption]`, or `[Judgement]`)
  3. **Options** (2 or 3 options with trade-offs, risks, and what would change the answer)
  4. **Recommendation and what to measure next**
- **Research mode** (toggle the **Research** pill in the composer): combines internal read-only checks with outside public web sources (when enabled by an admin), citing outside links and dates separately from Welile figures.
- **Memory that learns (with full user control)**:
  - If Zoe notices a repeated formatting or focus preference (such as *"in millions"*, *"Kampala Central"*, or *"short answers"*), she offers once: **"Remember this?"** with **Save** and **No thanks**. Nothing is ever saved without your tap.
  - Open the **`...` menu -> Zoe notes** to view, edit, or delete your notes, toggle **Memory off**, or switch on **Temporary chat** (which reads and writes no memory or history).
- **Opt-in Morning Brief (*What changed*)**: enable the in-app Morning Brief to see 2 to 3 lines for your role comparing today with the 4-week average, each with its official source, a direct link to the screen, and a one-tap mute switch.
- **Files only when you ask**: Zoe never clutters your chat with unrequested downloads. When an answer has a table, tap the small **Excel (.xlsx)** or **PDF** chip—or ask *"give me an Excel"* / *"PDF please"*—to download a clean file.

---

## 2. What Zoe Will Never Do (Safety Rules)

- **Zoe never changes Welile business data.** She cannot approve a requisition, move money, edit a wallet or ledger row, or change a Rent Plan, Supporter portfolio, tenant, or landlord record. If you ask her to change data, she will name the exact Welile screen to use instead.
- **Zoe never sends an email or notification without your click.** When you ask her to email a report, set a reminder, notify a colleague, or save an idea, she shows a draft card first so you can review and click **Send** or **Save**.
- **Zoe only shows data your role is allowed to see.** Staff roles require two-step login (authenticator app) before Zoe reads department reports.

## 4. Giving Feedback or Reporting a Problem

Under every Zoe answer (or in the message action bar) you can:
- **Copy** the answer text.
- Tap **Thumbs up** when the answer is accurate and helpful.
- Tap **Thumbs down** or **Report a problem** to send a redacted item to the weekly manager review queue (`CRM -> Zoe Staff Assistant -> Weekly Learning`), where managers can promote real edge cases directly into Zoe's regression evaluation suite.
