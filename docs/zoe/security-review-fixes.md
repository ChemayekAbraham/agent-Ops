*Date*: 27 October 2026  
*Reference*: `docs/ZOE_POST_IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md` (Prompt 1 — Findings `H1`, `H3`, `H4`, `H5`, `M9`, `M10`, `M12`, and all Low items)
