## 1. Overview: What a Welile Rent Plan Costs

A Welile Rent Plan removes the anxiety of finding a large monthly lump sum for rent. Instead of struggling to pay your landlord at the end of the month, Welile pays your landlord their full agreed rent on your behalf. You then repay that amount in predictable, micro-payments that fit your income.

The cost of a Rent Plan is straightforward, fixed at the start, and transparent:

1. You agree on your plan duration and review the exact cost breakdown before confirming.
2. The total repayment amount shown on your confirmation screen is the final amount you pay.
3. Every payment you make reduces your remaining balance until your plan reaches zero.
4. There are no surprise variable charges, hidden processing fees, or fluctuating interest rates added during repayment.

## 2. The Three Cost Components of Every Rent Plan

Your total repayment consists of three distinct components: the rent amount, the request fee, and the access fee.

### Component 1: The Rent Amount

The rent amount is the contractual monthly figure agreed between you and your landlord.

* **What you enter:** When creating a Rent Request, you or your agent enter the exact monthly rent figure specified in your tenancy agreement.
* **What Welile pays:** Welile disburses 100% of this figure directly to your landlord, fully settling your rental obligation for the period.
* **The period covered:** A standard Rent Plan covers one monthly rental period (30 days of tenancy).
* **Important distinction on duration:** Selecting a longer repayment period—such as 60 or 90 days—gives you more time to repay your plan, but it still covers that single month of landlord rent. A longer plan does not pay your landlord for multiple months of rent.
* **Payment timing:** Submitting a Rent Request does not instantly disburse money to the landlord. Funds are released only after your application passes identity checks, phone verification calls with both you and your landlord, and final financial approval.

### Component 2: The Request Fee

The request fee is a standard administrative charge that covers tenant onboarding, identity verification, property mapping, and underwriting.

* **Tier 1 (Rent of UGX 200,000 or less):** The request fee is exactly **UGX 10,000**.
* **Tier 2 (Rent above UGX 200,000):** The request fee is exactly **UGX 20,000**.
* **Unambiguous threshold:** A rent of exactly UGX 200,000 falls in Tier 1 and pays UGX 10,000. Any rent starting at UGX 200,001 or higher falls in Tier 2 and pays UGX 20,000.
* **Included in your total:** You do not pay the request fee out of pocket upfront. It is included in the quoted total repayment and spread evenly across your daily installments.
* **Terminology across the platform:** Depending on where you look in the app, this charge may be labelled as the "Request Fee," "Registration Fee," or "Platform Fee." These names all describe this single charge. You are never billed for a registration fee, request fee, and platform fee separately on the same plan.
* **Charged per plan:** This charge applies once per Rent Plan application. It is not a lifetime account fee. When you complete a plan and renew for your next month of rent, a new request fee applies to that new plan.

### Component 3: The Access Fee

The access fee is the charge for accessing capital upfront and spreading your rent into flexible daily installments over time.

* **What it covers:** It provides the return to the financing partners who fund your rent upfront and supports ongoing platform operations.
* **What affects the fee:** The access fee depends directly on your rent amount and your chosen repayment duration.
* **The effect of duration:** A longer repayment period increases the access fee because funds are deployed for a longer time. For example, a 60-day plan incurs a higher access fee than a 30-day plan for the same rent amount.
* **Minimum pricing rule:** Shorter repayment durations remain subject to the platform's minimum access charge rule, ensuring operational viability and partner return guarantees.
* **Not a penalty:** The access fee is an agreed, transparent financing cost determined when your plan is created. It is never a late penalty or overdue fee.

## 3. How Total Repayment Works in Plain Language

Your total repayment is simply the sum of your landlord's rent, your request fee, and your access fee for your chosen duration.

When your Rent Plan is approved:

* **Total Repayment:** This is the complete amount you owe for the entire plan.
* **Daily Repayment:** This is your total repayment divided evenly across the number of days in your plan, rounded up to the nearest whole shilling.
* **Remaining Balance:** This shows the total repayment minus all payments confirmed on your account so far.
* **Amount Due Now:** This represents today's scheduled installment plus any missed installments from previous days. It is not your entire remaining balance unless your plan has reached its final day.
* **No double charging:** Your daily payments already include the request fee and access fee. You are never asked to pay additional service fees alongside your regular installments.
* **Mobile Money charges:** Standard network transfer charges assessed by MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money are separate telecom transaction fees paid to your telecom operator when sending funds.

## 4. Verified Real-World Pricing Examples

The following examples illustrate how the three components form the total repayment and daily payments under verified standard pricing.

### Example A: Rent of UGX 200,000 Repaid Over 30 Days

* **Landlord Rent:** UGX 200,000
* **Request Fee:** UGX 10,000
* **Access Fee:** UGX 66,000
* **Total Repayment:** UGX 276,000
* **Daily Repayment:** UGX 9,200 per day

### Example B: Rent of UGX 200,000 Repaid Over 60 Days

* **Landlord Rent:** UGX 200,000
* **Request Fee:** UGX 10,000
* **Access Fee:** UGX 153,780
* **Total Repayment:** UGX 363,780
* **Daily Repayment:** UGX 6,063 per day

### The Key Trade-Off Explained

Comparing Example A and Example B illustrates how duration affects your plan:

1. **Lower daily payment:** Choosing 60 days reduces your daily payment from UGX 9,200 to UGX 6,063, making each day's installment easier to afford from daily earnings.
2. **Higher overall cost:** The total repayment increases from UGX 276,000 to UGX 363,780. The 60-day plan costs UGX 87,780 more in total because the funds are deployed for twice as long.
3. **Core rule:** Spreading your rent over more days does not simply divide the same total cost over a longer period. A longer plan gives you daily breathing room, but increases the total access fee.

*Note: These examples represent standard verified pricing as of 22 September 2026. Your confirmed plan details provide your exact personal amounts and schedule.*

## 5. Repayment Duration, Frequency, and Payment Amount

Understanding the difference between duration, frequency, and payment amount prevents missed payments and confusion:

* **Repayment Duration:** The total time frame of your plan, such as 30 days or 60 days.
* **Payment Frequency:** How often payments are scheduled. Welile Rent Plans operate on an active daily schedule. An installment is due every day during the plan.
* **Payment Amount:** The amount you choose to submit at any given time.

### Using the "1 Week" Payment Shortcut
On the payment screen, you may see shortcuts such as "1 Day," "3 Days," or "1 Week." 

* Selecting "1 Week" pays seven daily installments in a single transaction for convenience.
* Using this shortcut does **not** convert your plan into a weekly schedule where you can wait until the end of the week to pay.
* Unless you have paid installments in advance, daily payments are expected every day.

## 6. Pre-Confirmation Review Checklist

Before you or your agent tap confirm on a Rent Request, verify each of the following items on your confirmation screen:

1. **Rent Amount:** Matches the exact rent owed to your landlord.
2. **Housing Period:** Confirms that one month of tenancy is covered.
3. **Repayment Duration:** Shows the number of days you agreed to (e.g., 30 or 60 days).
4. **Payment Schedule:** Shows daily due dates.
5. **Request Fee:** Correctly reflects UGX 10,000 or UGX 20,000 based on your rent tier.
6. **Access Fee:** Displays the fee for your chosen duration.
7. **Total Repayment:** Clearly displays the full sum of rent, request fee, and access fee.
8. **Daily Installment:** Displays the exact daily payment required.
9. **First Payment Date:** Indicates the day your first installment is due.
10. **Final Payment Date:** Indicates the target date to reach a zero balance.
11. **Payout Details:** Confirms the landlord's verified phone number and name.

## 7. Important Edge Cases & Payment Rules

### Whole-Shilling Rounding
Daily payment amounts are rounded up to the nearest whole shilling to avoid fractional currency issues. Your final payment on the last day automatically adjusts downward so that you never pay more than the agreed total repayment.

### Partial Payments
If you pay less than the full daily installment, the amount is immediately credited against your remaining balance. However, your daily obligation is only considered complete once the full scheduled installment for that day has been received.

### Paying Ahead
You can pay more than your daily installment at any time. When you pay ahead, extra funds cover upcoming daily installments in sequence, moving your next required payment date forward. Paying ahead reduces your balance faster, but does not alter the total repayment agreed upon at confirmation.

### Early Settlement
You may settle your entire remaining balance early at any time. Paying off your plan clears your account immediately and unlocks eligibility for a new Rent Plan renewal. Early payment does not retroactively change or refund the agreed access fee unless an explicit promotional rebate applies.

### Missed Payments and Overdue Balances
If you miss a daily payment, the missed amount carries forward as overdue arrears. Welile does not assess arbitrary late penalty fees, but an overdue balance affects your platform trust rating and limits future rent coverage until cleared.

### Renewals on Completed Plans
When a Rent Plan is completed, you can renew for the next month with a single tap. Because each renewal represents a brand-new monthly rent payment to your landlord, a new request fee and a new access fee apply to the new plan.

### Payment Troubleshooting
If your Mobile Money payment does not reflect within two minutes, navigate to the troubleshooting guide at `/troubleshooting/payments` to check transaction matching or enter your telecom payment reference.

## 8. Application-Display Gap Report (RentFlow Insights Audit)

A review of the current RentFlow Insights production interface reveals the following display characteristics:

### Current Screen Behavior
On the **Your Rent Plan** screen, users currently see:
* Total Plan Amount (representing total repayment).
* Amount Paid to date.
* Remaining Balance.
* Daily Repayment figure.
* Plan start and end dates.
* Recent payment history.

### Identified Gap
The current interface does not separately display the original landlord rent, the request fee, or the access fee once the plan is active.

### Product Recommendations
1. **Add a "Your Cost Breakdown" Card:** Introduce an expandable section on the Rent Plan screen displaying the stored landlord rent, request fee, and access fee so tenants can review their breakdown at any time.
2. **Clarify Labels:** Replace ambiguous headers such as "Rent Plan Amount" with "Total Repayment" to prevent users from confusing the total plan cost with the landlord's monthly rent.
