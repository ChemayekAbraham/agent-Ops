# Agent communication — the new landlord float flow

**Ready to send. Three versions for three channels.**
Effective Monday 28 September 2026.

Terminology checked: "Rent Plan" throughout, never "loan".

---

## 1. SMS / WhatsApp — the short one

Use this if it goes out by SMS. Kept tight so it does not run to three billed
segments.

```
Welile: NEW from Monday 28 Sept 📢

When the CFO sends landlord float to your wallet, you have 24 HOURS to pay the
landlord.

⏰ Reminders at 6hrs and 18hrs.
⚠️ Not paid in 24hrs and no payout raised? The float returns and the tenant's
Rent Plan is cancelled.
✅ Tried and the payment failed? Nothing happens to you. We handle it.

Once the landlord is paid you get an SMS with the RECEIPT NUMBER 🧾, the day
your tenant starts, and your 1% commission 💰.

📅 Your tenant starts repaying the DAY AFTER the landlord is paid.
🕕 Payouts run 06:00-22:00.
```

## 2. WhatsApp broadcast / group post — the full one

This is the one to use if you have a group. It explains the *why*, which is
what stops the questions.

```
📢 WELILE AGENTS — IMPORTANT UPDATE

Starting Monday 28 September, the landlord payout has a clock on it.
Please read this once, properly. 🙏

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💰 WHEN THE CFO SENDS YOU LANDLORD FLOAT

The money lands in your landlord float wallet, earmarked for that one
landlord and that one tenant. You will get an SMS the moment it arrives 📲

From that moment, you have 24 HOURS to pay the landlord. ⏰

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⏰ WE WILL REMIND YOU — TWICE

• After 6 hours — a reminder if the landlord is still unpaid
• After 18 hours — a warning, about 6 hours left

Nothing is sent between 10pm and 6am 🌙 — payouts are closed then anyway, so
we will not disturb you.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠️ WHAT HAPPENS IF 24 HOURS PASS

If you NEVER raised a payout at all:
❌ The float is returned
❌ The tenant's Rent Plan is cancelled
❌ The tenant is told nothing is owed by them, and you can submit again

If you DID raise a payout and it FAILED:
✅ NOTHING happens to you
✅ No penalty. A network or merchant failure is not your fault
✅ A Welile officer is alerted and will sort it out with you

We built it this way on purpose. An agent who did their job will never be
punished for a failed network. 🤝

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🧾 WHEN YOU PAY THE LANDLORD — WHAT YOU GET

The landlord enters their OTP, the payment goes through, and Financial
Operations confirms it. Then you get an SMS with:

🧾 THE RECEIPT NUMBER — the same one the landlord receives. Yours to keep, to
   show, and to quote if anyone asks
📅 The exact day your tenant starts repaying
💵 The daily amount they will pay you
💰 Your 1% commission on the amount you paid out — already in your
   withdrawable balance

Your landlord gets their own SMS too, with the receipt number and a link they
can open to see it. ✅

Please still upload the receipt in the app. 📸

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📅 WHEN THE TENANT STARTS REPAYING

Your tenant starts repaying the DAY AFTER the landlord is actually paid.

Not the day after approval. Not the day after the CFO releases the money.
The day after YOUR LANDLORD HAS THE MONEY IN HAND. 🏠

This is a change in your favour. Before, a tenant could be marked as owing for
days while the landlord was still waiting — and you would open your app and
find them already behind through no fault of yours. That is finished. ✅

Your tenant also gets a welcome SMS that same moment telling them the date,
the daily amount, the total, and that YOU will be collecting from them. 📲

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🕕 REMEMBER

• Payouts run 06:00 – 22:00 only
• Pay the landlord, then submit the TID and receipt
• The 24 hours start the moment the money hits your wallet — not the next
  morning
• Any float already in your wallet from before Monday is NOT affected

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Questions? Call Welile Support on 0748747134 ☎️

Thank you for the work you do. 🙏
— Welile
```

For a screen or a printed sheet at a service centre, where emojis matter less
and clarity matters more.

```
NEW: THE 24-HOUR LANDLORD PAYOUT
From Monday 28 September 2026

WHEN THE CFO SENDS YOU LANDLORD FLOAT
The money arrives in your landlord float wallet for one named landlord and one
named tenant. You get an SMS immediately. You have 24 hours to pay the
landlord.

REMINDERS
6 hours: a reminder. 18 hours: a warning with the time remaining.

WHEN THE TENANT STARTS
The day after the landlord is actually paid. Not the day after approval, and

PAYOUT HOURS
06:00 to 22:00.

FLOAT ALREADY IN YOUR WALLET
Float released before Monday 28 September is not affected by the 24-hour rule.

Support: 0748747134
```

## Notes for whoever sends this

**What is deliberately not in it.** Nothing about commission rates changing.
The only commission mentioned is the 1% at landlord-paid, stated as what the
agent receives, not as a change from anything.

**Send it before Monday.** The rule starts at midnight on the 28th. An agent who
learns about a deadline after it has already cost them a tenant will not
believe the next thing we tell them.

**The one thing to be ready for.** Agents holding float right now — there are
42 such cases, some four months old — will ask whether the clock applies to
them. It does not. The rule only covers float released from Monday onward.
Those older cases are being worked through by a person, case by case.

**One inconsistency to close first.** The SMS an agent receives when float lands
does not currently mention the 24-hour deadline — that sentence was deliberately
left out while the rule did not yet exist. From Monday it does exist, so the
message should say so. Worth fixing before this goes out, otherwise the
broadcast promises a warning the SMS does not give.
