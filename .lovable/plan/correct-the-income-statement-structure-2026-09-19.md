- Replace the duplicated service-summary-plus-statement layout with one clear multi-step Income Statement.
- Keep revenue grouped by Welile service, but place it inside the formal revenue section.
- Show revenue deductions immediately after gross revenue, followed by **Net Revenue**.
- Present **Cost of Revenue**, then **Gross Profit** and Gross Margin.
- Present operating expenses once, with marketing included in that section rather than as a separate profit stage.
- Present the earnings sequence correctly: **EBITDA → Depreciation & Amortization → Operating Profit (EBIT) → Finance Income/Costs → Profit Before Tax → Tax → Net Income**.
- Move expected-versus-realized revenue recognition beneath the completed Income Statement as a supporting schedule, so it cannot be mistaken for another revenue total.
- Preserve the review block only when genuinely unmapped entries exist.

- Correct the EBITDA/EBIT presentation so depreciation is deducted between EBITDA and EBIT, not added after an already-labelled EBIT subtotal.
- Keep adjustments visibly separate and show exactly where they affect earnings.
- Preserve every underlying ledger classification and amount; this work changes report presentation and subtotal sequencing, not transactions.
