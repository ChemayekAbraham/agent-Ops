# Standardize Balance Sheet Breakdown Modals

## Change
- Apply the same concise breakdown format to every Asset, Liability, and Shareholders’ Equity item.
- Keep the selected item’s total at the top.
- Show only the named underlying components and their exact UGX values.
- Remove value-source notes, ledger account activity, recent entries, and other explanatory detail from these modals.
- Preserve all existing statement calculations, classifications, and accounting records.
