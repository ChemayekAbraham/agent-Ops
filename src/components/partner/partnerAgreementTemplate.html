3.4 Monthly Top-ups. The Partner may increase the Principal by making additional contributions in accordance with this Clause 3.4:

  • (a)
    Each additional contribution (a “Top-up”) may be made in any Return Period, and shall be made through an Approved Payment Channel within the period beginning five (5) days before and ending five (5) days after the last day of a Return Period (that last day being a “Return Date”, and that period the “Top-up Window”).
  • (b)
    A Top-up made within a Top-up Window shall be added to the Principal or, under Option B, to the Accumulated Balance, with effect from the start of the Return Period that begins immediately after the relevant Return Date. The Monthly Return for that Return Period and each later Return Period shall be calculated on the increased amount.
  • (c)
    Each Top-up forms part of the Contribution and the Principal for all purposes of this Agreement, including Clauses 7 and 9. A Top-up does not extend the Term or change the Return Option.

3.5 Multiple Partner Accounts. The Partner may hold more than one partner account with the Company. Each partner account shall be governed by a separate Tenant Partnership Agreement with its own Contribution, Commencement Date, Term and Return Option, and each account shall be administered independently of the others.

4. RETURN ON CONTRIBUTION

4.1 Rate. The Partner shall earn a Monthly Return of fifteen per cent (15%) for each Return Period, calculated in accordance with the Return Option elected under Clause 4.2.

4.2 Return Options. On signing this Agreement, the Partner shall elect one of the following Return Options:

  • (a)
    Option A – Monthly Payout. The Monthly Return is calculated on the Principal only and is paid out to the Partner at the end of each Return Period. It does not compound, and the Principal remains unchanged throughout the Term. Over a full twelve (12) Return Periods, the Monthly Returns amount to one hundred and eighty per cent (180%) of the Principal, in addition to the repayment of the Principal itself.
  • (b)
    Option B – Compounding. The Monthly Return is calculated on the Accumulated Balance and is not paid out. Instead, at the end of each Return Period it is added to the Accumulated Balance, so that each following Monthly Return is calculated on the increased balance. Over a full twelve (12) Return Periods, the Accumulated Balance grows to approximately 5.35 times the Principal.

4.3 Election binding for the Term. The Return Option elected in the Execution section of this Agreement shall apply for the entire Term and may not be changed before the Expiry Date. A Partner who elects Option B may not withdraw Monthly Returns during the Term, and may access the Accumulated Balance before the Expiry Date only by giving a Withdrawal Notice under Clause 9. If no Return Option is elected, Option A shall apply.

4.4 Common terms. Under either Return Option, the Monthly Return does not vary with the performance of any particular Tenant or tenancy, and each Monthly Return is rounded to the nearest Uganda Shilling.

7. GUARANTEE, RISK AND LIABILITY

7.1 The Company guarantees the repayment of the full Principal and the payment of all Monthly Returns due under this Agreement, including, under Option B, all Monthly Returns added to the Accumulated Balance.

7.2 The Company bears full liability for, and shall absorb, any loss, delay or default by any Tenant. No such loss, delay or default shall reduce, suspend or delay the Partner’s entitlement under this Agreement.

7.3 Where a payout to the Partner is delayed by external factors, the Company shall promptly notify the Partner of the delay and shall resolve it within three (3) Business Days.

8. TERM AND RENEWAL

8.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date of signature by both Parties and shall remain in force for a period of one (1) year from the Commencement Date (the “Term”), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Clause 11.

8.2 Either Party shall give the other written notice regarding renewal of this Agreement not less than three (3) months before the Expiry Date.

9. WITHDRAWAL OF PRINCIPAL

9.1 The Partner may withdraw the Principal by giving the Company a Withdrawal Notice not less than ninety (90) days before the intended withdrawal date.

9.2 Upon receipt of a Withdrawal Notice, the Company shall have a principal recovery period of ninety (90) days (the “Recovery Period”) within which to recover the funds from active use and repay them to the Partner.

9.3 No Monthly Return shall accrue or be payable during the Recovery Period, the funds no longer being in active use.

9.4 The Company shall repay to the Partner, not later than the last day of the Recovery Period, the Principal in full or, under Option B, the Accumulated Balance as at the date the Withdrawal Notice was received.

10. DEFAULT

10.1 A Party is in default if it fails to make any payment due under, or otherwise fails to comply with, this Agreement.

10.2 The defaulting Party shall have fourteen (14) days from receipt of written notice of the default from the other Party within which to remedy it.

10.3 If the default is not remedied within that period, the non-defaulting Party may take legal action for breach of contract, subject to Clause 12.

11. TERMINATION

11.1 The Partner may terminate this Agreement by giving a Withdrawal Notice under Clause 9, in which case Clauses 9.2 to 9.4 apply.
