WELILE MERCHANT AGENT AGREEMENT

(Cash-Out Agent — Float Distribution & Payout Services)

1) PURPOSE OF THIS AGREEMENT

1.1 This Agreement is entered into between Welile Technologies Limited (“Welile”, “the Company”, “we”, “us”) and the Merchant Agent, {{MerchantName}} (“Merchant Agent”, “Cash-Out Agent”, “you”), for the platform welile.com, effective {{EffectiveDate}} (Version v{{Version}}). By clicking “I Agree”, you confirm that you have read, understood, and accepted this Agreement in full. This Agreement sets out the terms under which you act as a Welile Merchant Agent (also called a Cash-Out Agent).

1.2 Your core role is simple:

  • Welile gives you FLOAT (company money) to hold and distribute.
  • Users on the platform request to withdraw money from their wallets.
  • You pay those users in cash (or mobile money) using the float Welile provided.
  • Welile pays you a commission for each payout you complete and verify.

1.3 You are a trusted distribution point for the Company's money.

The float is NOT yours — it belongs to Welile at all times until it is correctly paid out to a verified user.

2) RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES (NOT AN EMPLOYEE)

2.1 You are an independent merchant partner, NOT an employee of Welile.

2.2 This Agreement does NOT create:

  • a salary or wage entitlement
  • employee benefits (NSSF, pension, leave, insurance)
  • an employment relationship
  • any authority to bind Welile in contracts with third parties

2.3 You are responsible for your own:

  • business location and operating hours
  • transport, airtime, data, and running costs
  • taxes and statutory obligations on your commission income

3) THE FLOAT (COMPANY MONEY HELD IN TRUST)

3.1 OWNERSHIP: All float advanced to you remains the property of Welile. You hold it strictly in trust (“on behalf of the Company”) for the sole purpose of paying out approved user withdrawals.

3.2 PERMITTED USE: Float may ONLY be used to:

  • pay out verified, platform-approved user withdrawal requests, and
  • be returned/reconciled to Welile on request.

3.3 PROHIBITED USE: You must NEVER use float for:

  • your own personal expenses or business
  • lending money to other people
  • any payment not generated and approved through the Welile platform
Using float for any unauthorised purpose is a serious breach and may be treated as misappropriation of company funds.

3.4 FLOAT BALANCE: Your float balance is tracked in the platform at all times. Every payout you make reduces your float; every top-up increases it. The platform ledger is the final record of how much float you hold.

3.5 TOP-UPS & LIMITS: Welile decides how much float to advance you and may increase, reduce, freeze, or recall float at any time based on your performance, volume, and risk profile.

4) HOW A PAYOUT WORKS

4.1 A user requests a withdrawal from their wallet on the platform.

4.2 You CLAIM the request in the app before paying — this locks the request to you so two agents cannot pay the same user.

4.3 You verify the user (4-digit withdrawal code / user identity) before releasing any cash.

4.4 You pay the user the exact approved amount using your float.

4.5 You submit proof of payment (reference, screenshot, or confirmation) in the app.

4.6 The platform then:

  • debits the float you used against the company money you hold, and
  • credits your COMMISSION for that payout.

4.7 Both you and the user receive an SMS confirming the payout and the user's remaining wallet balance.

5) COMMISSION & SETTLEMENT

5.1 COMMISSION RATE: You earn a commission of 0.5% of the value of each successfully completed and verified payout.

5.2 PRINCIPAL RECONCILIATION: Where you pay a user out of your own mobile-money/cash and not from pre-loaded float, the full principal you paid is reimbursed to your withdrawable wallet, plus the 0.5% commission. You never lose your principal on a correctly executed, verified payout.

5.3 SEPARATION OF FUNDS:

  • FLOAT WALLET — company money you hold to distribute. It is NOT income and is NOT withdrawable as your own money.
  • COMMISSION / WITHDRAWABLE WALLET — your earned commission and reimbursed principal. This is YOUR money.

These wallets are kept strictly separate. Float can never be reclassified as your earnings.

5.4 NO COMMISSION ON INVALID PAYOUTS: Commission is only earned on payouts that are claimed, verified, paid, proven, and approved through the platform. Unverified, duplicated, or self-paid transactions earn nothing and may be reversed.

5.5 RATE CHANGES: Welile may revise the commission rate from time to time for policy, sustainability, fraud-risk, or market reasons. Changes are communicated within the platform.

5.6 REVERSALS: Welile may withhold or reverse commission where fraud, error, or an unresolved dispute is detected.

6) MERCHANT AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES

You agree to:

  • keep enough float available to serve users during your operating hours
  • pay users promptly once a request is claimed and verified
  • always verify the user's withdrawal code / identity before paying
  • submit accurate proof of every payout
  • reconcile your float honestly and keep your records matching the platform ledger
  • keep the float safe and secured against theft or loss
  • treat users courteously and professionally

7) LIABILITY FOR FLOAT (KEY SECTION)

7.1 FULL ACCOUNTABILITY: You are fully accountable for all float advanced to you. At any time, the float you hold PLUS the verified payouts you have made must equal the float Welile advanced to you.

7.2 SHORTFALLS: Any unexplained shortfall, missing float, or money paid out without valid platform approval and proof is YOUR personal liability and becomes a debt owed by you to Welile.

7.3 RECOVERY OF DEBT: Welile may recover any float shortfall or wrongful payout by:

  • deducting from your commission / withdrawable wallet,
  • deducting from future earnings,
  • recalling remaining float, and/or
  • lawful debt-recovery and legal action where necessary.

7.4 PAYING WITHOUT VERIFICATION: If you pay a user without a valid claimed request, without the correct withdrawal code, or without proof, you do so at your OWN RISK and you bear the full loss.

7.5 THEFT / LOSS OF FLOAT: You are responsible for safeguarding the float in your custody. Loss due to your negligence, robbery without report, or mishandling is your liability. Genuine incidents must be reported to Welile and the authorities immediately.

8) PROHIBITED CONDUCT (ZERO TOLERANCE)

You must NEVER:

  • charge users extra/unauthorised fees on top of their withdrawal
  • pay a user less than the approved amount and keep the difference
  • use float for personal or third-party purposes
  • fake payout proof or create false transactions
  • pay yourself or related accounts to harvest commission
  • share user data, codes, or contacts externally
  • collude with users or other agents to defraud the platform

Violations may result in:

  • immediate termination and float recall
  • forfeiture of pending commission
  • full personal liability for losses
  • reporting to authorities and legal action

9) DATA PROTECTION & CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 You must keep all user and platform information strictly confidential.

9.2 You must not store, share, post, or sell user IDs, phone numbers, codes, or payout details outside the platform. Breach may result in termination and legal action.

  • stop accepting new payout requests,
  • reconcile your account, and
  • return ALL remaining float held to Welile.

10.3 Outstanding float or debts remain payable after termination.

11) LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

11.1 Welile is not responsible for:

  • your personal accidents, injuries, or business losses
  • losses arising from your own negligence, fraud, or breach
  • device loss or local operating costs

11.2 Welile's responsibility is limited to crediting commission and reimbursing principal correctly for valid, verified, platform-approved payouts.
