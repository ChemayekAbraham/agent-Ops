EARLY ANGEL POOL SHAREHOLDERS
AGREEMENT

1. Purpose of the Agreement

This Agreement establishes and governs the terms under which individuals participate in the Welile Technologies (U) Ltd Early Angel Pool. The purpose of this structure is to provide an accessible and organized investment channel for early-stage supporters of the Company, enabling them to collectively contribute capital while aligning with the Company’s long-term growth strategy. This Agreement ensures transparency, fairness, and clarity regarding the rights, obligations, and expectations of all participants within the pool.

2. Angel Pool Structure

Welile Technologies (U) Ltd has created an Early Angel Pool with a maximum capitalization of UGX 500,000,000 (Five Hundred Million Uganda Shillings). This pool represents up to eight percent (8%) of the Company’s total equity. The allocation of this equity is reserved specifically for early angel investors who participate under this Agreement, allowing the Company to raise capital while maintaining a streamlined ownership structure.

3. Participation Model

Participation in the Early Angel Pool is structured as a pooled investment mechanism. Individual contributions made by participants are aggregated into a single investment pool, and each participant’s economic interest is calculated proportionally based on their financial contribution relative to the total pool size. This model simplifies administration, reduces complexity in share distribution, and allows smaller investors to participate meaningfully in the Company’s growth.

4. Clarification of Angel Pool Ownership

The Early Angel Pool, as a collective entity, holds up to 8% ownership in Welile Technologies (U) Ltd. Rather than issuing separate share certificates to each participant, the pool is treated as a single block of equity for operational simplicity. Each participant retains a proportional economic interest within this block, corresponding to their share of the total pool contribution.

For illustration purposes, if a participant contributes ten percent (10%) of the total pool value, they are entitled to an economic interest equivalent to 0.8% of the Company’s total equity. This structure ensures clarity in ownership while maintaining efficiency in corporate governance.

5. Rights of Participants

Participants in the Early Angel Pool are entitled strictly to economic rights derived from their proportional ownership. These rights may include profit participation, value appreciation, and potential exit proceeds. However, participants do not possess voting rights, decision-making authority, or direct control over the Company’s operations, management, or strategic direction. All governance and operational decisions remain exclusively with the Company’s leadership.

6. Dividend Policy

Welile Technologies (U) Ltd is currently in a growth and reinvestment phase. As such, the Company does not anticipate issuing dividends for a minimum period of four (4) years from the date of this Agreement. During this period, all generated revenues and profits will be reinvested into business expansion, product development, and market penetration efforts to maximize long-term shareholder value.

7. Future Corporate Structure

The Company reserves the right to restructure the Early Angel Pool in the future, including but not limited to converting participant interests into a global holding entity, special purpose vehicle (SPV), or other investment structure. Such restructuring may be undertaken to enhance scalability, facilitate international investment, or align with future funding rounds, and will be executed in a manner that preserves the proportional economic interests of all participants.

8. Governance and Control

All strategic, financial, and operational decisions of Welile Technologies (U) Ltd shall remain under the sole authority of the Company’s founders, directors, and appointed management. Participation in the Early Angel Pool does not grant any governance rights or influence over Company decisions. This centralized governance model is intended to ensure agility, consistency, and effective execution of the Company’s vision.

9. Risk Disclosure

Participants acknowledge and fully understand that this investment constitutes a high-risk, long-term commitment. Early-stage ventures inherently carry significant uncertainty, and there is no guarantee of returns, profitability, or liquidity. Participants are encouraged to carefully evaluate their financial capacity and risk tolerance before committing funds, as they may lose part or all of their investment.
