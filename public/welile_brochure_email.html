THE CHALLENGE

Many people earn money every day, every week or through different income streams.
But rent often requires a large payment at once.

Income comes in small amounts. → Rent comes in large amounts.
Welile helps bridge this gap.
HOW WELILE WORKS
1
1. TENANT
A tenant applies for rent support through Welile.
2
2. VERIFICATION
Welile verifies the tenant, landlord and rental information.
3
3. RENT PAYMENT
Welile facilitates payment of the approved rent to the landlord.
4
4. REPAYMENT
The tenant repays Welile through manageable instalments according to the agreed arrangement.
5
5. TRACKING
Payments are recorded digitally, creating a reliable history of the tenant's rental behaviour.
