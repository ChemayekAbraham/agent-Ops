HOW WELILE WORKS

1

TENANT APPLIES

Tenant applies for rent support through the Welile platform or a Welile service centre.

2

VERIFICATION

Welile verifies the tenant, landlord and rental information to ensure transparency and trust.

3

RENT PAYMENT

Welile facilitates payment of the approved rent to the verified landlord.

4

REPAYMENT

The tenant repays Welile in small, manageable instalments based on the agreed schedule.

5

TRACKING & RECORDS

Payments are recorded digitally, creating a reliable rental behaviour history that builds financial identity.
